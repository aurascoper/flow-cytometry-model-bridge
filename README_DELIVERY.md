## Scientific boundary
Flow cytometry is treated as an observation layer for sampled cell populations (`:effluent`, `:planktonic`, `:detached_biofilm`). It does not directly measure in situ pore-scale spatial structure, EPS cross-link density, mineral phases, or corrosion rates. In Track 1 (`:track1_nanobio`), the concentric reaction-diffusion shells are explicitly tagged as `:predicted` simulated concentration fields rather than observed physical layers unless independently verified by imaging. In Track 3 (`:track3_bitumen_rheology`), the wireframe-over-core visualization represents a bitumen emulsion droplet and its interface under shear without concentric biological release shells. The software adapters and their `739/739` combined passing unit assertions (`118/118` + `621/621`) verify mathematical consistency, synthetic parameter recovery, calibration algebra, group-aware validation, and data-contract enforcement; they do not substitute for empirical wet-lab calibration or coupled multiphysics validation.
