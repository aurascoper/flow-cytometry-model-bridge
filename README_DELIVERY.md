## Scientific boundary
Flow cytometry is treated as an observation layer for sampled cell populations (`:effluent`, `:planktonic`, `:detached_biofilm`). It does not directly measure in situ pore-scale spatial structure, EPS cross-link density, mineral phases, or corrosion rates. In Track 1 (`:track1_nanobio`), the concentric reaction-diffusion shells are explicitly tagged as `:predicted` simulated concentration fields rather than observed physical layers unless independently verified by imaging. In Track 3 (`:track3_bitumen_rheology`), the wireframe-over-core visualization represents a bitumen emulsion droplet and its interface under shear without concentric biological release shells. The software adapters and their `1,067/1,067` combined passing unit assertions (`262/262` + `805/805`) verify mathematical consistency, synthetic parameter recovery, calibration algebra, conservative anisotropic mesh/speciation coupling, Menke et al. (Oct 2026) benchmark parity, switchable biofilm constitutive/regulatory dynamics, Droop quota conservation, controlled $R_0 \dots R_5$ ablations, group-aware validation, and data-contract enforcement; they do not substitute for empirical wet-lab calibration or coupled external production solver validation (`empirical_generalization_established: false`, `integrated_into_existing_openfoam_phreeqc_solvers: false`).
